03 / The process

From your idea to finished art.

A straightforward commission process with clear payment and progress updates.

1

Send a request

Tell me what you want and add a reference photo. A request is not yet an approved price.

2

Confirm the details

I review your request and confirm the final price and the details with you.

3

Pay 50% to start

Pay the downpayment by GCash. I verify it, then begin. Follow your artwork progress from your customer account.

4

Pay the balance, receive it

Pay the remaining balance (plus the LBC shipping fee if sent by courier). Local delivery can be paid in cash on delivery.
